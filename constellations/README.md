# Constellations Screensaver

An animated star map that pans across the sky, zooms in on one of the 88 constellations, and shows a card with its meaning, when it is best seen, whether it can be seen from Pennsylvania, its brightest star and that star's distance.

Live page: https://eric15774.github.io/spreadsheets/constellations/screensaver/

Click or press Space to skip ahead. Press F for full screen.

## Data sources

- Stars (about 5,000, magnitude 6 and brighter): [HYG Database v3.8](https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database) by David Nash, CC BY-SA 4.0
- Constellation lines and boundaries: [d3-celestial](https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial), copyright (c) 2015 Olaf Frohn, BSD 3-Clause License
- "Best seen" is the month the constellation is highest in the south at about 9 PM. "From Pennsylvania" uses a latitude of 40.8° N.
